## Full description

> You don't have a loneliness problem. You have a logistics problem.
>
> You have contacts, events, and good intentions. What's missing is the system that turns them into an actual social life: going out, following up, and coming back to the same rooms until familiar faces become friends.
>
> Orbit is that system.
>
> **Tonight** — The Solo Night Generator gives you three plans sized to your energy and budget. The easy one still counts as a rep.
>
> **Events** — Add events, and Orbit scores them — weighting recurring ones, because becoming a regular is how weak ties become friends. Get arrival strategies, openers, and an exit rule so you're never trapped.
>
> **People** — A private tracker for who you met and where. Orbit warns you when a tie is going cold, drafts a follow-up in your voice, and hands it off to your own messages. It never sends anything for you.
>
> **Reps & Review** — Log the small reps (left the apartment, talked to one person, sent a follow-up). See a weekly review built from what you actually did — including the pattern you've been avoiding.
>
> **30-Day Rebuild** — Four weeks: leave the apartment, start conversations, follow up, build cadence. Miss a day and you continue — never restart.
>
> Private by default. No feed, no profiles, no performing. Orbit never reads your messages, never auto-sends, and never imports your contacts.
>
> Not a dating app. Not a social feed. Not therapy. Not an AI companion. A system for adults rebuilding a social life on purpose.
