# Screenshots Plan

Seven screenshots, ordered as a narrative: the promise, then the loop, then the payoff. Each has a caption overlay (short, benefit-led). Capture at 390px-class device width against seeded demo data so screens look alive, not empty.

| # | Screen | Route | Caption overlay |
|---|--------|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Landing / hero | `/` | "Build a real social life in 30 days." |
| 2 | Dashboard | `/app` | "The next move is always obvious." |
| 3 | Solo Night Generator (3 plans) | `/app/tonight` | "Three plans for tonight. The easy one counts." |
| 4 | People + follow-up draft | `/app/people/[id]` | "Drafts that sound like you. You hit send." |
| 5 | 30-Day Program | `/app/program` | "Four weeks. Miss a day, continue — never restart." |
| 6 | Weekly Review | `/app/review` | "See the pattern you've been avoiding." |
| 7 | Pricing | `/pricing` | "Free to start. Private by default." |

## Order rationale

1 sells the promise; 2 shows the app makes action obvious; 3–4 show the two halves of the loop (go out / follow up); 5–6 show the payoff (structure + insight); 7 handles the money question last, after value is established.

## Production notes

- Use the seeded demo (Omar, Grove Street Run Club, Jersey City) so no real data appears and screens are populated.
- Dark, calm palette — do not brighten for the store; the muted look is the brand.
- Keep captions to one line; let the UI carry the rest.
- The follow-up screenshot (#4) must show the conditional handoff row, since "you hit send, not us" is a key differentiator and a review-safety point.
- Never screenshot real CRM data. Ever.

## Localization

Launch English-only. The seed locale (JC/NYC) is fine for v1; city packs (future) would drive localized screenshot sets later.
