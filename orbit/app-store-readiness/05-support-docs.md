## FAQ

**Is my data private?**
Yes. In demo mode, everything you enter stays in your browser/device and is never sent anywhere. No accounts, no feed, no sharing. See our privacy note in Settings.

**Does Orbit send messages for me?**
No — and it never will. Orbit drafts follow-ups and hands them off to your own WhatsApp, SMS, or email. You tap send. Orbit never reads or sends your messages.

**Does Orbit import my contacts?**
No. You add people manually. Orbit never touches your contact book.

**Is this a dating app?**
No. Dating/marriage momentum is one goal you can pick, but Orbit is about building a social life, not matching you with strangers.

**Is this therapy or a mental-health app?**
No. Orbit is a behavior and planning tool. It makes no medical or mental-health claims and isn't a substitute for professional support.

**How do I export my data?**
Settings → Export JSON. You get a file with everything, which you can re-import anytime.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings → Delete all local data. This removes everything from your device. There's no undo, so export first if you want a backup.

**How do I reset to the sample data?**
Settings → Reset to sample data. This restores the demo (Jersey City/NYC seed).

**Why is the AI free / does it need an API key?**
The suggestions are generated on your device — no external AI service, no API key, no data sent out. It's built so a real AI model could be added later without changing how your data is handled.

**What do the paid tiers do?**
In this version, payments aren't active — paid features are marked to show where the product's paid edge will sit. The free tier is fully usable.

## Delete-data instructions (standalone, for the listing)

> To delete all your Orbit data: open the app → **Settings** → **Delete all local data** → confirm. This permanently removes everything stored on your device.

## Export-data instructions (standalone)

> To export: open the app → **Settings** → **Export JSON**. Save the file somewhere safe. To restore later, use **Import JSON** and select that file.
