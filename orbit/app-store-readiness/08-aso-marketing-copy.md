## Keyword bank

Primary: social, friends, make friends, adult friendship, social life
Secondary: meetup, introvert, follow up, community, routine, social skills, weak ties, loneliness (as topic, never a claim), new city, remote work
Long-tail: how to make friends, things to do alone, social plan, 30 day plan, become a regular

Fit within the 100-char keyword field; prioritize primary + highest-intent secondary. Don't stuff; Apple penalizes irrelevance.

## App preview video script (15–30s)

> [Open on a dark, calm phone screen — the dashboard.]
> **VO / text:** "You don't need more events."
> [Tap Tonight → three plans appear.]
> "You need one plan for tonight."
> [Tap a person → a follow-up draft appears, then the handoff row.]
> "And a way to actually follow up. You hit send — Orbit never does."
> [Show the 30-day program, day counter ticking.]
> "Thirty days. Miss one, you continue — never restart."
> [End card: Orbit logo.]
> "Orbit. Build a real social life. Private by default."

Keep it quiet and confident — no upbeat stock music, no frantic cuts. The calm *is* the pitch.

## Product-page optimization test ideas

- Test subtitle A vs. B (transformation vs. category) on conversion.
- Test screenshot #1 = hero vs. #1 = dashboard (promise-first vs. product-first).
- Test promotional-text variant 1 (feature-led) vs. 2 (pain-led).
- Test icon variants only if there's a real hypothesis — don't churn the icon; users find it by shape.
