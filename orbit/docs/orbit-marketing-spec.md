43. Marketing Strategy and Go-To-Market System

Orbit should not only be built as an app. It should be built as a product that can be launched, explained, tested, and monetized.

Add a full marketing and go-to-market package to the repo.

Create a folder called /marketing or /launch/marketing.

The marketing system should include:

1. Positioning Strategy

Create a positioning document that clearly defines:

* Product name: Orbit
* Category: Personal social operating system
* Core promise: "Build a real social life in 30 days."
* One-line description:
    "Orbit gives you weekly outings, follow-up prompts, and a private relationship tracker so you stop starting from zero every weekend."
* Primary buyer:
    35+ introverted or socially underbuilt professionals rebuilding social life
* Primary pain:
    They have intentions, contacts, and events, but no repeatable system for turning weak ties into real relationships.
* Primary transformation:
    From isolated, reactive, and avoidant to socially active, consistent, and intentional.
* Main behavior loop:
    Pick event → attend → talk to one person → capture weak tie → follow up → repeat.

Also define what Orbit is not:

* Not a dating app
* Not a friend-matching app
* Not a social feed
* Not an AI companion
* Not therapy
* Not a generic CRM
* Not another productivity app

2. Messaging Pillars

Create 5 messaging pillars:

A. Stop wasting nights

* Pain: User ends up at home scrolling despite wanting a better life.
* Promise: Orbit gives one concrete action for tonight.

B. Weak ties become relationships through follow-up

* Pain: User meets people but lets the connection die.
* Promise: Orbit tracks who to follow up with and what to say.

C. Social life needs reps, not vibes

* Pain: User waits to feel confident.
* Promise: Orbit treats social rebuilding like training mileage.

D. Recurring exposure beats novelty

* Pain: User keeps trying random events but never becomes known anywhere.
* Promise: Orbit pushes recurring anchors.

E. Private by default

* Pain: Social apps feel performative or creepy.
* Promise: Orbit is a private operating system, not a feed.

For each pillar, create:

* Landing page headline
* Short paragraph
* Feature proof
* Example CTA

8. Launch Plan

Create a launch plan with phases.

Phase 0: Private personal use
Goal:
Use Orbit personally for 7 days and fix obvious friction.

Tasks:

* Complete onboarding
* Generate 7-day plan
* Add real events
* Add real people
* Use follow-up generator
* Complete weekly review
* Note where app feels fake, annoying, or too much

Phase 1: Friends and trusted testers
Goal:
Get 5–10 people to use demo mode.

Tasks:

* Send private URL
* Ask them to complete onboarding
* Ask them to generate a plan
* Ask what felt useful/confusing
* Track drop-off points

Phase 2: Public waitlist
Goal:
Validate positioning.

Tasks:

* Launch landing page
* Add email capture
* Publish 3 SEO articles
* Post 5 LinkedIn posts
* Share in appropriate communities carefully
* Collect waitlist signups

Phase 3: Paid smoke test
Goal:
Validate willingness to pay.

Options:

* $9/month Pro mock checkout
* $29 one-time 30-Day Social Rebuild Plan
* $49 city/persona pack
* $99 concierge social rebuild audit

Phase 4: Productized launch
Goal:
Launch Pro with real payments only after behavior loop works.
