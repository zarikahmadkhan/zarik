# Positioning

## The one-liner

**Orbit is a private personal social operating system for adults rebuilding their social life.**

Core promise: *Build a real social life in 30 days.*

One-line description:
> Orbit gives you weekly outings, follow-up prompts, and a private relationship tracker so you stop starting from zero every weekend.

## The category

Orbit defines a category rather than joining one: **personal social operating system.** Not an event app, not a CRM, not a habit tracker — the layer that sits on top of all of those and produces *behavior*. The category framing matters because every existing tool the buyer has tried is a discovery tool or a storage tool. Orbit is an action tool.

## Who it's for

**Primary buyer:** 35+ introverted or socially underbuilt professionals rebuilding social life in or near a major city. High-functioning, often high-income, socially under-practiced. They do not want to identify as "lonely" — they want to identify as *rebuilding*.

**Primary pain:** they have intentions, contacts, and events, but no repeatable system for turning weak ties into real relationships. The bottleneck is not information. It is cadence and follow-through.

**Primary transformation:** from isolated, reactive, and avoidant → to socially active, consistent, and intentional. Note the framing: this is a transformation of *behavior and rhythm*, never a promised emotional or relational outcome.

## The behavior loop (everything ladders to this)

1. Pick something worth attending.
2. Leave the apartment.
3. Stay long enough to create a chance of connection.
4. Talk to one person.
5. Capture the weak tie.
6. Follow up.
7. Return to recurring settings.
8. Build cadence.

## What Orbit is NOT

State these plainly and often — the negative space is as important as the promise:

- **Not a dating app.** (Dating/marriage momentum is one goal among several, never the product's center.)
- **Not a friend-matching app.** Orbit doesn't introduce you to strangers; it helps you act on the people and events already in your life.
- **Not a social feed.** No posts, no likes, no performing.
- **Not an AI companion.** The AI drafts and suggests; it never talks *to* people for you or pretends to be a friend.
- **Not therapy or a mental-health app.** No clinical language, no treatment claims.
- **Not a generic CRM.** It answers "should I follow up and what do I say," not "log this contact."
- **Not another productivity dashboard.** Its only metric is real-world social reps.

## Core differentiation, in one sentence

> Every other tool helps you discover infinite options. Orbit helps you choose one, attend it, follow up, and repeat.

## Proof it can back this up (shipped in v0.1)

- Solo Night Generator: three concrete plans for tonight, hard-filtered to your avoided settings
- Recurring-weighted event scoring (repeated exposure is the mechanism, not novelty)
- Private people tracker with reality-check warnings, including a two-unanswered-messages stop rule
- Follow-up drafts with why-it-works, risk level, and handoff (never auto-send)
- Social rep tracker, weekly review built from real data, and a 30-day rebuild program with non-restart recovery
