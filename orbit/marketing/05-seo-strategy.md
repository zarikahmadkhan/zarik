# SEO Strategy

Orbit uses SEO **carefully and helpfully**. The entire strategy is premised on quality, not volume.

## Guardrails (read first — these are hard rules)

- **Do not** mass-generate thin pages.
- **Do not** create city pages without real local insight.
- **Do not** create near-duplicate pages with swapped city names.
- **Do not** publish any page whose only purpose is ranking.
- **Every** page includes genuinely useful advice a reader would value even if Orbit didn't exist.
- **Every** city page includes real local context: actual neighborhoods, event types, and a neighborhood strategy.
- **Every** page drives toward one useful Orbit action: generate a solo plan, start the 7-day plan, add an event, generate a follow-up, or start the 30-day rebuild.

If a page can't clear the "would this help someone who never signs up?" bar, it doesn't get published.

## Five full article outlines

### 1. How to Make Friends After 35 Without Using Another Social App
- Hook: the couple-friends / moved-away / busy problem; why 35+ is structurally harder.
- Section: it's logistics, not likability.
- Section: the mechanism — recurring exposure beats novelty (cite the "become a regular" idea).
- Section: the follow-up gap where every weak tie dies.
- Section: a concrete 2-week starter (pick one recurring event, attend twice, one follow-up).
- Close: soft intro to Orbit as the system that runs this. CTA: 7-day plan.

### 2. Things to Do Alone in Jersey City When You Want to Get Out of the Apartment
- Hook: restless-but-drained, don't want a whole production.
- 12 real outings across Journal Square, Grove St, the waterfront, Manhattan-adjacent — sorted quiet → social-adjacent.
- Callout: which of these can turn into conversation if you let them.
- The one-micro-goal idea (ask one person one question).
- Close: the Solo Night Generator does this for any mood/budget. CTA: Solo Night Generator.

### 3. How to Follow Up After Meeting Someone at a Run Club
- Hook: the specific awkwardness of the "we ran together, now what" moment.
- Timing: the 24-hour window.
- Three scripts (casual, low-pressure, the direct invite to the next run).
- The stop rule: two unanswered messages, then let it rest.
- Close: Orbit drafts these and tracks who's owed. CTA: follow-up generator.

### 4. The 30-Day Social Rebuild Plan for Introverts
- Hook: the all-or-nothing trap introverts fall into.
- Week 1: leave the apartment. Week 2: start conversations. Week 3: follow up. Week 4: build cadence.
- The exit-rule concept and why it removes the dread.
- Non-restart recovery: missing a day isn't failure.
- Close: Orbit runs the whole program with recovery built in. CTA: 30-day rebuild.

### 5. Why Recurring Events Beat Random Meetups for Adult Friendship
- Hook: you've been to 20 events and have zero friends — here's why.
- The mere-exposure effect, in plain language.
- The "three visits" threshold where regulars start recognizing you.
- How to pick an anchor and commit to it.
- Close: Orbit's scoring weights recurring events for exactly this reason. CTA: score your events.
