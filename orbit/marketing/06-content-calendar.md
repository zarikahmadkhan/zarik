# 90-Day Content Calendar

Channels: LinkedIn · X/Twitter · Reddit (cautiously) · SEO blog · email newsletter · short-form video scripts · landing-page updates.

Per-week template (hold this cadence every week):
- 1 SEO article
- 3 LinkedIn posts
- 3 short social posts (X/threads)
- 1 email newsletter
- 1 product-led post (shows the tool doing something)
- 1 user story or scenario (clearly fictional until real ones exist)

Tone across everything: direct, clear, adult, slightly contrarian. Never cheesy, therapy-speak, hustle-bro, pickup-artist, or dating-app-coded. See `14-brand-voice.md`.

## Reddit rule

Reddit is in the mix but **cautiously**. Never drop links cold. Participate genuinely in r/introvert, r/[city], r/socialskills, divorce-recovery subs; mention Orbit only when someone is explicitly asking for a tool and it genuinely fits, and disclose you built it. One spammy post can burn a community permanently. When in doubt, don't.
