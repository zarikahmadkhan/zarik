# Launch Plan

Five phases, gated. Do not skip ahead — each phase de-risks the next. The whole plan is built to **not** spend money on ads or scale until the behavior loop demonstrably works, starting with the founder.

## Phase 0 — Private personal use
**Goal:** use Orbit personally for 7 days and fix obvious friction. *(This is the current step per `PLAN.md` — and the reason the marketing folder's felt-claims are marked "tune after.")*

Tasks:
- Complete onboarding as yourself.
- Generate the 7-day plan; add real events for the week.
- Add real people you meet; use the follow-up generator for real.
- Complete a weekly review.
- Note everywhere the app feels fake, annoying, or like too much.

Exit criteria: you've used every core flow on real data and have a written friction list.

## Phase 1 — Friends and trusted testers
**Goal:** 5–10 people use demo mode.

Tasks:
- Send the private URL to people who fit the personas.
- Ask each to complete onboarding and generate a plan.
- Ask what felt useful vs. confusing.
- Track where they drop off.

Exit criteria: at least half complete onboarding and generate a plan without help; you've logged the top 3 confusion points.

## Phase 2 — Public waitlist
**Goal:** validate positioning.

Tasks:
- Launch the landing page publicly with email capture.
- Publish the first 3 SEO articles (Cluster 2 and 5 lead).
- Post 5 LinkedIn posts from the Month-1 calendar.
- Share in appropriate communities carefully (see Reddit rule).
- Collect waitlist signups.

Exit criteria: a signal that the positioning resonates — meaningful signup rate from cold traffic, qualitative "this is exactly my problem" replies.

## Phase 3 — Paid smoke test
**Goal:** validate willingness to pay *before* building real billing.

Options (mock checkout — measure intent, don't charge yet, or use a simple Stripe payment link if you want real signal):
- $9/mo Pro
- $29 one-time 30-Day Social Rebuild
- $49 city / persona pack
- $99 concierge social-rebuild audit

Exit criteria: a real click-through-to-pay rate on at least one offer. If nobody tries to pay, fix the product or positioning before building billing.

## Phase 4 — Productized launch
**Goal:** launch Pro with real payments — **only after the behavior loop works** (for you and testers).

Tasks:
- Implement Stripe behind the existing pricing UI.
- Turn on the real gates (the mocked ones already mark the edges).
- Scale content and, only now, consider paid ads (see `10-paid-ads.md`).

Sequencing principle from the spec: *payments before the behavior loop works is a mistake.* Don't invert this.
