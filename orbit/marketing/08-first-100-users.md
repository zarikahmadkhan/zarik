# First 100 Users

The first 100 come from warm, targeted, non-spammy outreach — not ads. Channel by channel, with the angle, what *not* to say, an example message, spam risk, and the CTA.

## Channels

### Personal LinkedIn
- **Why it fits:** the buyer (35+ professionals) lives here; the founder story is credible here.
- **Angle:** build-in-public, "I built a CRM for my social life."
- **Don't say:** anything that sounds like an MLM pitch or a guaranteed-results promise.
- **Example:** "I got tired of losing every good conversation to no follow-up, so I built a private system for my own social life. Sharing it if it's useful." + link.
- **Spam risk:** Low, if it's genuinely personal.
- **CTA:** Try the demo.

### Newsletter / personal audience
- **Why it fits:** already-warm, already-trusting.
- **Angle:** honest founder note + the friction you're solving for yourself.
- **Don't say:** over-polished launch-speak; keep it human.
- **Spam risk:** Very low.
- **CTA:** Try the demo, reply with feedback.

### Reddit (r/introvert, r/socialskills, r/[city], divorce-recovery)
- **Why it fits:** exact-pain communities.
- **Angle:** participate genuinely; share the tool only when someone asks for one.
- **Don't say:** never cold-drop a link; never post the same thing across subs.
- **Example:** in a thread asking "how do you follow up without being weird" → give the real advice, then "I actually built a small tool that drafts these; happy to share if useful."
- **Spam risk:** HIGH. One wrong move burns the community. Disclose you built it.
- **CTA:** Soft, only on request.

### Local NYC / Jersey City groups
- **Why it fits:** the seed locale; city-specific relevance.
- **Angle:** "a tool for actually building a routine here."
- **Don't say:** don't spam neighborhood boards.
- **Spam risk:** Medium.
- **CTA:** Try the demo / read the JC guide.

### Divorce-recovery communities
- **Why it fits:** persona #1, high pain, high fit.
- **Angle:** rebuilding without shame; never a dating pitch.
- **Don't say:** absolutely no romance/dating framing here.
- **Spam risk:** High (sensitive space). Lead with empathy and usefulness.
- **CTA:** Read the "after divorce" article, then the demo.

### Remote-worker & introvert communities
- **Why it fits:** personas #3, #4.
- **Angle:** the office/energy problem, structured reps.
- **Spam risk:** Medium.
- **CTA:** Try the demo.

### Running clubs & event groups
- **Why it fits:** persona #6; a recurring anchor made of strangers.
- **Angle:** turn the faces you already see into friends.
- **Spam risk:** Low if you're a member.
- **CTA:** Score your recurring events.

### Indie hackers / build-in-public
- **Why it fits:** supportive early adopters, feedback-rich.
- **Angle:** transparent build + metrics.
- **Spam risk:** Low.
- **CTA:** Try it, tell me what's fake.

### Friends of friends, alumni groups, professional networks
- **Why it fits:** trust travels.
- **Angle:** personal ask.
- **Spam risk:** Low.
- **CTA:** Test it and be honest.

## Outreach scripts

**Ask a friend to test:**
> "I built a thing for my own social life — a private system for actually going out and following up with people. Would you try it for 10 minutes and tell me where it feels fake or annoying? No pressure, just want a real reaction."

**LinkedIn post:**
> "Adult friendship is mostly logistics and repetition — and I was bad at both. So I built Orbit: a private operating system for rebuilding a social life. Weekly outings, follow-up drafts, a tracker for the people you actually want to stay in touch with. It's free to try, no account. Would love your honest take."

**Local group post:**
> "Made a small free tool for building an actual social routine in [city] — recurring events, follow-ups, that kind of thing. Sharing in case it's useful to anyone else trying to get out more. Happy to hear what's missing."

**DM to a potential beta tester:**
> "Hey — you mentioned wanting to get out more / meet more people. I've been building something for exactly that and I'd love a real tester. Ten minutes, totally private, and I mostly want to know what annoys you. Up for it?"

**To someone recently moved or divorced (careful, warm):**
> "No agenda here — I've been building a tool for rebuilding a social life from scratch, and you came to mind. It's private and low-key. If you ever want to try it, it's here. Either way, hope you're settling in."

**Waitlist invite:**
> "Orbit's opening up soon. It's a private system for building a real social life in 30 days — outings, follow-ups, a tracker. Want on the early list?"

## What "100" looks like

Aim for 100 people who *complete onboarding and generate a plan*, not 100 signups. The metric that matters (see `11-analytics-metrics.md`) is activation, then whether they come back in week 2.
