# Paid Ads Test Plan

**Do not prioritize ads before organic validation.** This plan exists so it's ready, not so it runs early. Paid spend starts only after the landing page shows a clear conversion rate from organic/warm traffic (Phase 2–3 in `07-launch-plan.md`).

## Channels (in eventual priority order)

1. Apple Search Ads (later — once there's an app)
2. Google Search ads (later — highest intent)
3. Meta ads (later — good persona targeting, worse intent)
4. Reddit ads (later — cheap, community-adjacent)

## Initial budget

$100–$500 total, **only after** landing-page conversion is demonstrated. Treat it as a measurement expense, not growth spend.

## Test angles

Each angle maps to a persona and a landing variant. Success metric is demo-start rate, then 7-day-plan generation.

| Angle | Persona | Headline | Body | CTA | Landing variant |
|-------|---------|----------|------|-----|-----------------|
| Make friends after 35 | post-divorce, high-performer | "Adult friendship is mostly logistics." | "You have the intentions. Orbit is the system — weekly outings, follow-ups, a 30-day rebuild." | Start your 7-day plan | B (transformation) |
| Things to do alone tonight | introvert, remote | "You wanted to go out. Here's where." | "Three plans for tonight, sized to your energy and budget. The easy one still counts." | Try the Solo Night Generator | A (direct) |
| Rebuild after divorce | post-divorce | "The couple-friends are gone. Your social life isn't." | "Rebuild your rhythm one outing and follow-up at a time. Private, no shame, no dating pitch." | Start your 30-day rebuild | B (transformation) |
| Introvert social plan | introvert | "You don't need to become an extrovert." | "Small reps, an exit rule, one conversation at a time. A paced 30-day plan." | Try the Solo Night Generator | C (private OS) |
| New city friend plan | new-city | "New city, still no one to call?" | "Turn a new city into a real routine — recurring anchors and a follow-up system." | Find your anchors | B (transformation) |
| Stop wasting weekends | remote, high-performer | "Another weekend home, scrolling?" | "Orbit gives you one concrete plan for tonight and keeps the week moving." | Start your 7-day plan | A (direct) |

## Rules

- Every ad obeys the claim guardrails: no "find friends/love/your people guaranteed."
- Kill any angle that doesn't beat organic CAC within the test budget.
- Never run the dating-adjacent angle (#8 persona) as a romance promise — it sells the social life, full stop.
- Measure to the *activation* event (plan generated), not the click.
