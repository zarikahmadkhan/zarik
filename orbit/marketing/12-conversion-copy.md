# Conversion Copy

The rule: **focus on outcomes, not features.** People don't pay to maintain a database or get "more AI generations." They pay to keep a working social rhythm from collapsing.

These strings back the upgrade modals already shipped in the app (`components/UpgradeModal.tsx`, sourced from `generateUpgradePrompt`). Keep app copy and marketing copy in sync with this file.

## Upgrade triggers (where the paid edge sits)

- Finishes the 7-day plan
- Hits 3 solo plans in a month
- Wants the full 30-day rebuild (program week 2+)
- Wants unlimited follow-up drafts
- Wants the advanced weekly review
- Wants a city / persona pack
- Wants an exportable strategy

## Good vs. bad, side by side

| Don't | Do |
|-------|-----|
| "Upgrade for more AI generations." | "Keep your weekly social plan going." |
| "Unlock CRM features." | "Don't let the people you met disappear." |
| "Access advanced analytics." | "See where your social life is actually stalling." |
| "Get the full program." | "Week one was motion. The next three weeks are where it sticks." |
| "Unlimited solo plans." | "Every restless night gets a plan." |
| "Premium tier available." | "A plan built for your city and your situation." |

## Trigger-by-trigger copy

**Finished 7-day plan:**
> Week one is motion. Weeks two through four are where weak ties become people you actually know. The 30-day rebuild continues from exactly where you are. → *Continue with Pro*

**Solo plan limit (3/month):**
> That's three nights you didn't spend scrolling. Pro makes the generator unlimited — every restless night gets a plan. → *Unlock unlimited plans*

**Program continuation:**
> Leaving the apartment was week one. Conversations, follow-ups, and cadence are what you'd be stopping short of. → *Continue the rebuild*

**Unlimited follow-ups:**
> Every unsent follow-up is a weak tie dying quietly. Pro drafts as many as your orbit needs. → *Unlock follow-up drafts*

**Advanced review:**
> The advanced review names your patterns — what you avoid, what returns energy, which settings actually produce friends. → *Upgrade for the full review*

**City / persona packs:**
> Jersey City anchors, post-divorce pacing, new-city resets — packs tune the whole system to your actual life. → *Browse packs with Premium*

## Honesty rule

While payments are mocked (v0.1), every upgrade surface says so plainly. Don't fake scarcity, fake urgency, or fake social proof. The paid edges are marked to be honest about where the product's line is, not to trick anyone across it.
