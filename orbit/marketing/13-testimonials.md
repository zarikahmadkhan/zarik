# Testimonials & Social Proof

**Every quote in this file is a placeholder. Do not present any of them as real.** Fabricating testimonials is dishonest and, for the App Store, a rejection risk. Mark them clearly wherever they appear (the landing page's social-proof slot must render a visible "example" label until real quotes exist).

## Placeholder themes (what real quotes should eventually sound like)

These capture the transformation in the user's own imagined voice — use them as *targets* for what to collect from real Phase-1 testers, not as copy to publish:

- "I stopped treating every weekend like a blank page."
- "The follow-up prompts were the part I actually needed — I always meant to text people and never did."
- "It helped me become a regular somewhere, which I'd never managed before."
- "I realized I was collecting events instead of attending them."
- "The reality check told me to stop chasing someone who wasn't responding. I needed to hear it."
- "Week one just got me out of the apartment. That was enough to start."

## How to collect real ones (Phase 1)

- Ask testers the specific question: "What did Orbit get you to do that you wouldn't have done otherwise?" The answer *is* the testimonial.
- Get explicit permission and decide attribution (first name + city, or anonymous).
- Prefer specific over glowing: "I sent three follow-ups I'd have skipped" beats "great app!"

## Rules

- Never fabricate a real person or quote.
- Never imply a guaranteed outcome, even in a testimonial ("I made 5 friends in a month" is a claim-guardrail violation even from a real user — prefer behavior-focused quotes).
- Until real quotes exist, the landing page's social-proof area either stays empty or shows clearly-labelled example themes.
