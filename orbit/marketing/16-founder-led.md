# Founder-Led Marketing

Orbit is credible when it's positioned as built by someone who lived the problem — not by a growth team chasing a market. The founder story is the highest-trust distribution channel Orbit has.

## What the founder understands (and should speak from)

- Adult loneliness that coexists with a good career and a full inbox
- Rebuilding after divorce, a move, or a life phase that deleted the old social circle
- High-functioning isolation — the person who can run a team but not build a weekend
- The gap between *having options* and *taking action*
- The insight that social life needs reps, not motivation

The founder doesn't claim to be a guru. They claim to be someone who was bad at this, got systematic about it, and built the tool they needed.

## Building-in-public content ideas

Post these as genuine reflections, not campaigns:

- "I built a CRM for my social life because I was tired of losing weak ties."
- "The problem was never finding events. The problem was going."
- "Adult friendship is mostly logistics and repetition. I was bad at both, so I built a system."
- "Recurring events beat random novelty. I have the attendance data on myself to prove it."
- "Your social life needs reps, not motivation. Here's what a week of reps looks like."
- "I'm testing whether a 30-day plan can rebuild social momentum — starting with me."

## The narrative arc (for a launch thread or About page)

1. The honest admission: successful on paper, socially under-built, tired of wasting weekends.
2. The realization: it's not a personality problem, it's a systems problem.
3. The build: a private tool to run the loop — go, talk to one person, follow up, repeat.
4. The test: using it on myself first (Phase 0), then a few friends.
5. The invitation: it's free to try, private, and I want to know where it feels fake.

## Rules

- Never overclaim results ("I made 20 friends"). Speak to behavior and process ("I went from one social thing a month to two a week").
- Never turn the loneliness angle into pity-bait. The tone is matter-of-fact, slightly contrarian, useful.
- The founder is a practitioner sharing a system, not an influencer selling transformation.
- Keep it consistent with `14-brand-voice.md` — direct, calm, honest without being cruel to yourself either.
