# Orbit — Marketing & Go-To-Market

This folder is the go-to-market system for Orbit. It exists so the product can be launched, explained, tested, and monetized — not just built.

**Status:** written against the shipped v0.1 product. Structure is final; the felt-claims (which personas lead, which testimonial themes ring true, which pain points convert) should be tuned after the founder's own Phase-0 personal-use week, per `PLAN.md`. Nothing here should be published verbatim before that dogfood pass.

## Non-negotiable claim guardrails

Every document in this folder obeys these. They come from `CLAUDE.md` and the marketing spec's Definition of Done, and they are not optional:

- **No guaranteed outcomes.** Never promise friendships, dates, marriage, or mental-health improvement. Orbit sells a *system and a cadence*, not a result.
- **Not therapy, not a dating app, not an AI companion, not a feed.** Copy never drifts into any of these.
- **No shame.** Direct is fine; cruel is not. No "you're lonely and pathetic" framing, ever.
- **No alcohol-centric defaults.** Bars appear only as something Orbit helps you avoid. Faith/community events are first-class, not an afterthought.
- **Private by default.** Never imply Orbit shares, posts, or performs your social life.
