# Orbit Visual Style Guide (social)

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Ink 950 | `#0e0f11` | Primary background — every asset |
| Ink 900 | `#15161a` | Card surfaces |
| Ink 700 | `#282b31` | Hairline borders |
| Fog 50 | `#f2f2ef` | Primary text |
| Fog 400 | `#96988f` | Secondary text |
| Moss 500 | `#819561` | THE accent. Key words, progress, the orbit ring |
| Moss 300 | `#b9c5a2` | Accent on dark text blocks |
| Clay 400 | `#c18a67` | Secondary accent — sparingly, for the "mirror"/warning register |

Rule: one accent per asset. Moss by default; clay only when the content is a hard truth ("weak ties die"). Never both loud at once.

## Typography

- **Everywhere:** a bold grotesque. In-factory renders use Liberation Sans Bold (Helvetica metrics — exports cleanly). If editing in Canva/CapCut: Inter, Helvetica Now, or Arial Bold.
- Video on-screen text: 64–92px at 1080-wide, max 4 lines per scene, high contrast, centered-left.
- Carousel headline: ~76px; body 44–52px; generous margins (96px+).
- Uppercase + letter-spacing for the small kicker line ("ORBIT" / "SOCIAL REP OF THE DAY") only.

## Layout signature

- Dark field, text-first, lots of air.
- The orbit ring motif (thin moss circle + small clay satellite) appears small and consistent — bottom corner or above the kicker. Never decorative-huge.
- A single thin moss progress bar at the bottom of video frames (scene N of M) is the motion signature.
- No stock photos, no emoji in the image itself (captions may use sparingly), no drop shadows, no gradients louder than 5% luminance.

## What it should NOT look like

Therapy-app pastel, corporate slide, hustle-bro red/black, pickup-content thumbnails (arrows + shocked faces), AI-gradient purple. If it could be a Calm or a Gary-V asset, it's wrong. The reference feeling: a serious training log that respects the viewer.

## Files

- `wordmark.svg` — horizontal lockup (dark backgrounds)
- `avatar.svg` — square profile avatar, works at 100px
- App favicon/icon lives at `orbit/public/icon.svg` (same motif — keep consistent)
- Background pattern: solid ink with the ring motif at 4% opacity, generated by the templates — no separate texture file needed
