Slide 1: The 7 social reps adults actually need — Track them like training. Because that's what this is.
Slide 2: 1. Leave the apartment on purpose — Not errands. A chosen outing. Solo-but-public counts completely.
Slide 3: 2. Stay 45 minutes — Long enough for something to happen. Short enough to say yes to.
Slide 4: 3. Ask one person one question — “How did you find this event?” is the best opener in the building.
Slide 5: 4. Learn one name. Use it once. — Names are the cheapest investment in familiarity.
Slide 6: 5. Capture the weak tie — Write down who you met and where — before you forget the details that make follow-up easy.
Slide 7: 6. Send the follow-up within 72 hours — The rep everyone skips. The one that builds friendships.
Slide 8: 7. Return to the same place — Repeat exposure is the mechanism. The third visit changes everything.
Slide 9: One rep today. — The app tracks all seven — privately, no feed.
