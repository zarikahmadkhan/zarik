# Why weak ties die



## Slide 1
**Why weak ties die**

(and why it's nobody's fault)

## Slide 2
**A weak tie is someone you know a little — and like**

The run club person. The coworker from the old job. The friend-of-a-friend you clicked with.

## Slide 3
**Weak ties are where friendships come from**

Almost no one becomes your friend as a stranger. They pass through weak-tie territory first.

## Slide 4
**But weak ties have a half-life**

Every silent week, the odds decay. After a month, you're basically strangers with shared trivia.

## Slide 5
**Both people assume the other will reach out**

So neither does. The tie dies of symmetry.

## Slide 6
**The maintenance dose is tiny**

One low-pressure message. “Good meeting you — I'm going again next week.”
