# How to follow up after meeting someone once



## Slide 1
**How to follow up after meeting someone once**

Without being weird about it.

## Slide 2
**Rule 1: within 72 hours**

Day 3 they remember you warmly. Week 2 you need a reintroduction. Timing beats wording.

## Slide 3
**Rule 2: reference the place, not the person**

“Good meeting you at the run club” feels casual. “I really enjoyed meeting YOU” feels heavy. Same sentiment.

## Slide 4
**Script: the recurring anchor**

“Hey — good meeting you Thursday. I'm going again next week. You planning to be there?”

## Slide 5
**Script: the zero-pressure hello**

“Good meeting you the other day. No need to reply — just didn't want to be the person who says 'stay in touch' and never does.”

## Slide 6
**Rule 3: one message, then let it breathe**

If they don't answer, the door stays open. Two unanswered messages = stop.

## Slide 7
**The move is light + repeated**

Not intense + rare. Let the second meeting do the heavy lifting.
