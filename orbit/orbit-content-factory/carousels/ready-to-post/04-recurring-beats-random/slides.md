# Why recurring events beat random meetups



## Slide 1
**Why recurring events beat random meetups**

Every time. For everyone.

## Slide 2
**Random events start you at stranger-zero**

New room, new faces, full cold-start cost — every single time.

## Slide 3
**Recurring events compound**

Visit 1: stranger Visit 2: familiar face Visit 3: expected Visit 5: regular

## Slide 4
**“Expected” is the magic threshold**

People who are expected get greeted, get noticed missing, get invited to things.

## Slide 5
**The psychology is boring and bulletproof**

Mere exposure: we warm to people we've simply seen before. No charisma required.

## Slide 6
**The math favors you too**

10 random events = 10 cold starts. 10 visits to 2 anchors = 2 growing reputations.

## Slide 7
**Pick one thing. Go three times.**

Orbit scores your events and weights recurring ones on purpose.
