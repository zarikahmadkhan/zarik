Slide 1: The 30-day social rebuild plan — One assignment a day. No personality change required.
Slide 2: Week 1: Leave the apartment — Solo public outings. Low-pressure events. One 45-minute stay. Goal: motion, not meaning.
Slide 3: Week 2: Start conversations — Ask one question per outing. Learn one name. Talk to the organizer — they're paid in hellos.
Slide 4: Week 3: Follow up — Send the messages. Invite someone to a repeat event. Revive one dormant tie — no apology for the gap.
Slide 5: Week 4: Build cadence — Pick your 1-2 recurring anchors. Drop the low-yield stuff. Plan next month like training.
Slide 6: The only rule: never restart — Missed two days? Continue from today. Restarting is a trap disguised as discipline.
Slide 7: Day 30 you're not a new person — You're the same person with a working rhythm. That's the whole point.
Slide 8: The app runs the entire program — Daily assignments, recovery, progress — private, no feed.
