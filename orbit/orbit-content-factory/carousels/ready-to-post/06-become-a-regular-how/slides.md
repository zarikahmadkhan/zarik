# How to become a regular somewhere



## Slide 1
**How to become a regular somewhere**

The visit-by-visit playbook.

## Slide 2
**First: pick the right place**

Weekly (not monthly). Same people attend. Conversation is possible. You could imagine going 10 times.

## Slide 3
**Visit 1: just attend**

No networking agenda. Learn the rhythm of the thing. Find out when it repeats.

## Slide 4
**Visit 2: say “good to be back”**

To anyone who looks familiar — staff, organizer, the person who was there last week.

## Slide 5
**Visit 3: names**

Learn two. Use them. By now people have noticed you're becoming a fixture.

## Slide 6
**Visit 4-5: small contributions**

Arrive early, help set up, ask the organizer what they need. Helpers become insiders fast.

## Slide 7
**Then: the invitation happens on its own**

Regulars get invited to the after-thing. That's not luck — it's accumulated familiarity.

## Slide 8
**Track your repeat exposures**

Orbit counts them and nudges you back to your anchors.
