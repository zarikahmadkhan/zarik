# Stop collecting events you never attend



## Slide 1
**Stop collecting events you never attend**

A pattern you might recognize.

## Slide 2
**Saving feels like progress**

Dopamine of a plan, zero cost of attendance. That's the trap.

## Slide 3
**The saved folder is where intentions go to age**

4 events saved this month. 0 attended. Sound familiar?

## Slide 4
**Research is avoidance with a clipboard**

One more browse, one more comparison, one more “maybe next week” — all of it feels responsible. None of it is going.

## Slide 5
**The one-decision rule**

You can't save a new event until you've decided on the last one: going (pick the date) or not (delete it).

## Slide 6
**Options are not action**

One committed Tuesday beats a folder of perfect Saturdays.

## Slide 7
**Orbit makes you commit**

Interested → planned → attended. The statuses are the point.
