Slide 5: You need a rhythm — One recurring event, weekly. One follow-up, weekly. One ordinary outing at a time.
Slide 6: Private matters here — No feed. No announcing. No performing recovery for an audience.
Slide 7: Quiet. Repeatable. Yours. — Orbit runs the rhythm — nobody sees it but you.
