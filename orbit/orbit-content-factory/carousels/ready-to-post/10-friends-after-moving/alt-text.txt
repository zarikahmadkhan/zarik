Slide 1: Making friends after moving cities — The 90-day protocol.
Slide 2: First, the truth: — Nobody integrates you automatically. The city didn't notice you arrived.
Slide 3: Month 1: pick two recurring anchors — One fitness-ish, one interest-ish. Weekly, not monthly. Attend both twice.
Slide 4: Month 2: become a familiar face — Keep showing up. Learn names. Say “good to be back.” Talk to the organizers.
Slide 5: Month 3: convert — One follow-up per week to someone you've now met twice. Invite someone to the thing you already attend.
Slide 6: What NOT to do — Don't app-hop for strangers. Don't try 12 different events. Don't wait for work friends to materialize.
Slide 7: 90 days of this and the city knows your face — Not a big life. A real one. That's the start.
Slide 8: Orbit runs the protocol — Anchors, follow-ups, cadence — tracked privately.
