# How introverts can build social cadence



## Slide 1
**Social cadence for introverts**

No personality change required.

## Slide 2
**The worst advice: “put yourself out there”**

Out where? For how long? Doing what? Pressure isn't a plan.

## Slide 3
**Rule 1: structured beats open-ended**

A class, a club, a volunteer shift — things with a format and an end time. Mingling is the enemy.

## Slide 4
**Rule 2: the exit rule goes in first**

“I can leave after 45 minutes” is what gets you in the door. Knowing the exit makes the entrance possible.

## Slide 5
**Rule 3: one micro-goal per outing**

Ask one question. Learn one name. Then you're done — anything else is bonus.

## Slide 6
**Rule 4: recurring over novel**

Familiar rooms cost less energy. The 4th visit to the same place is cheaper than the 1st visit anywhere.

## Slide 7
**Rule 5: schedule recovery**

Social reps cost introverts real energy. Budget the recharge like part of the workout.
