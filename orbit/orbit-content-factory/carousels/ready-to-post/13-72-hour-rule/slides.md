# The 72-hour follow-up rule



## Slide 1
**The 72-hour follow-up rule**

Timing beats wording. Every time.

## Slide 2
**Met someone good? The clock is running.**

Day 1: they remember you clearly. Day 3: still warm. Week 2: stranger with shared trivia.

## Slide 3
**Why 72 hours works**

Soon enough that no reintroduction is needed. Not so fast it feels eager. The window does the work.

## Slide 4
**The message can be five words**

“Good meeting you at run club.” Done. The bar is that low because timing already cleared it.

## Slide 5
**Add one hook if you have one**

“I'm going again Thursday — you in?” The recurring event does the scheduling for you.

## Slide 6
**What NOT to send**

No essays. No intensity. No “we should definitely hang out sometime” — that's where plans go to die.

## Slide 7
**Then let it breathe**

One message. If no reply, the door stays open. Two unanswered = stop.

## Slide 8
**Orbit tracks the window and drafts the message**

You hit send. Always you.
