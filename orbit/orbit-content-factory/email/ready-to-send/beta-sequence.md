# Beta Tester Sequence — 5 emails

Trigger: someone agrees to beta test (DM/comment → email captured). Cadence: days 0, 1, 4, 8, 12. Goal: activation, honest feedback, and retention through week 2 — NOT conversion. Never pitch paid tiers to beta testers mid-sequence.

---

## Email 1 · Day 0 — The key
**Subject:** Your Orbit beta link (2 minutes to your first plan)
**Preview:** Free, no account, data stays in your browser.
**Segmentation:** all testers.

Thanks for testing Orbit. Here's your link: [LINK]

Two minutes of setup gets you a 7-day plan. Everything stays in your browser — no account, no contact import, nothing leaves your device.

The one thing I ask: when something feels fake, preachy, or annoying, tell me. That's the feedback that matters. Just reply to this email.

→ [Open Orbit]

---

## Email 2 · Day 1 — The first real test
**Subject:** Tonight's the actual test
**Preview:** Generate a solo plan with your honest energy level.
**Segmentation:** testers who onboarded (send to all if you can't segment yet).

The real test of Orbit isn't the onboarding — it's whether tonight's plan respects your actual state. Open Tonight, answer honestly (drained is a valid answer), and see if the minimum-viable plan feels doable rather than preachy.

If it suggests something you'd never do, reply and tell me what it got wrong. The generator is only as good as its misses.

→ [Plan tonight]

---

## Email 3 · Day 4 — The follow-up flow
**Subject:** Add one real person (this is the half that matters)
**Preview:** The tracker + draft + handoff flow, on a real weak tie.
**Segmentation:** all testers.

Half of Orbit is getting you out; the other half is what happens after. Add one real person — someone you've actually met recently — and generate a follow-up draft. Check three things:

1. Does the draft sound like a human (specifically: like you)?
2. Is the risk level honest?
3. Do the handoff buttons (copy / WhatsApp / SMS / email) feel right — including that Orbit never sends anything itself?

Reply with which of the six tones you used and whether you actually sent it. (No judgment either way — "I didn't send it because X" is the most useful sentence you can write me.)

→ [Open People]

---

## Email 4 · Day 8 — The week-2 question
**Subject:** One week in — the only question that matters
**Preview:** Did you do anything you wouldn't have done otherwise?
**Segmentation:** all testers.

One week. One question: **did Orbit get you to do anything you wouldn't have done otherwise?** An outing, a conversation, a follow-up — anything.

If yes: what was it? (That sentence is worth more to me than any feature request.)
If no: where did it lose you? Which screen did you close it on?

Also fair game: anything that felt like homework, shame, or an app trying too hard.

Reply either way — one line is plenty.

---

## Email 5 · Day 12 — Wrap + what's next
**Subject:** Beta wrap: what I'm fixing because of you
**Preview:** The change list, and whether you want to keep going.
**Segmentation:** all testers.

Beta round's wrapping. Here's what's changing based on this group's feedback: [FILL: 3-5 concrete changes with tester attribution like "several of you said X"].

Two small asks:
1. If Orbit earned it, keep using it — nothing expires, your data stays yours.
2. If one sentence of your experience is quotable (with your first name or anonymous), reply with it. Real words from real testers are the only social proof I'll ever use.

Thank you. This thing is better because you were blunt.

→ [Keep your rhythm going]
