# Post-Beta Feedback Sequence — 3 emails

Trigger: beta period ends (day ~14 after their start). Cadence: days 0, 5, 12. Goal: extract structured feedback, identify who's a real retained user, and (only in the final email, only to retained users) surface the paid smoke test.

---

## Email 1 · Day 0 — The exit interview
**Subject:** 4 questions, 2 minutes, brutal honesty invited
**Preview:** The only survey I'll ever send you.
**Segmentation:** all testers, active or lapsed.

The beta's done — thank you. Four questions, reply inline, fragments welcome:

1. What's the ONE thing Orbit got you to actually do?
2. What's the ONE thing that felt fake, preachy, or annoying?
3. If Orbit disappeared tomorrow: relieved, indifferent, or annoyed?
4. Would you introduce it to one specific person? (Who — a friend type, not a name?)

Question 3 is the one I care about most. "Indifferent" is a completely acceptable answer and the most useful one to explain.

---

## Email 2 · Day 5 — The lapsed-user email
**Subject:** No guilt — just curious where it lost you
**Preview:** One reply, one line, genuinely useful.
**Segmentation:** ONLY testers who stopped using the app (no activity in 7+ days). Skip entirely for active users.

You tried Orbit and drifted off — which is normal and fine, and I'm not here to win you back with a discount.

I'd just love one line: **what was the moment you closed it for the last time?** A screen, a suggestion that missed, life getting busy, the whole premise not fitting — anything. Lapsed-user honesty is the rarest feedback there is.

Reply with one line and I'll leave you in peace (and take you off the list if you'd like — just say so).

---

## Email 3 · Day 12 — The continue path
**Subject:** You're still using it — here's what's next
**Preview:** The roadmap, and the honest paid question.
**Segmentation:** ONLY retained users (active in the last week). Never send to lapsed testers.

You're one of the people still running the loop after the beta — which makes you exactly who I want to build for. Two things:

**The roadmap, shaped by this beta:** [FILL: accounts/sync, the top 2-3 requested fixes]. Your data carries forward through all of it.

**The honest paid question:** Orbit will eventually have a Pro tier (unlimited solo plans, the full 30-day rebuild, follow-up drafts for everyone in your orbit) around $9/mo. No pressure and no fake urgency — but if you'd genuinely pay for what you've been using, reply "I'd pay" and you'll get founding-user pricing when it goes live. If you wouldn't, reply "I wouldn't" — that's equally useful and changes nothing about your access.

Either way: thanks for being early.

→ [Keep going]
