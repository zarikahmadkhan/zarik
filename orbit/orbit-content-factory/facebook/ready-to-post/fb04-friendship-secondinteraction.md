# [adult-friendship] fb04-friendship-secondinteraction

## Main post
Something I keep coming back to: most adult friendships don't fail dramatically. They just never get a second interaction. We meet someone good, both say "we should hang out," and both wait for the other to text.

I got tired of losing people that way and built a small private tool that tracks who I met and nags me (kindly) to follow up within 72 hours. It's changed more than I expected. Looking for a few people to beta test — free, private, no account.
