# [divorce-recovery] fb07-divorce-couplefriends

## Main post
Something nobody warned me about: the social life was couple-shaped. The dinners, the trips, the standing plans — they came as a set, and after the divorce the logistics just quietly stopped including me. No malice anywhere. Still brutal.

What's helped is treating the rebuild as rhythm, not reinvention: one recurring thing weekly, one follow-up to someone new weekly, no announcements, no "new chapter" performance. Slow and unglamorous and it's working.

Sharing in case the frame helps someone else in the rebuild phase.
