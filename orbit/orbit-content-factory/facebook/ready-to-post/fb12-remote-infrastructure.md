# [remote-workers] fb12-remote-infrastructure

## Main post
Remote work realization that took me way too long: the office wasn't just where I worked — it was my ambient social infrastructure. Hallway hellos, lunch invites, birthday cake. Five to ten free micro-interactions a day, and I never counted them until they were gone.

Nothing replaces them automatically. You have to design the replacement: I do two recurring evening anchors a week + one solo-but-public work session + one follow-up text a week. That's the whole system and it works.

What did you build to replace the office's social side?
