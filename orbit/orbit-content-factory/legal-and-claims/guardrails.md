# Claims, Privacy & Optics Guardrails

Every asset in this factory was written against these. Every future asset must be too. Pre-publish check: grep your draft for the banned list below.

## Never say (banned claims)
- "cure loneliness" / "never be lonely again" / "fix your loneliness"
- "guaranteed friends" / "you'll make friends" / any promised social outcome
- "guaranteed dates" / "find love" / "meet your person" / anything romance-promised
- "therapy replacement" / "better than therapy" / therapy-coded language of any kind
- "mental health treatment" / "treats social anxiety" / "for depression" — no clinical claims, period
- "find your tribe" / "besties" / hustle-bro or pickup-artist phrasing
- Anything about the founder's employer — no naming, no hinting, no "at my day job at ___"
- "quit your job" / "startup escape" framing

## Always true (the only claims we make)
Behavior claims only: leave the apartment, talk to one person, follow up, become a regular, build a rhythm. We promise the loop, never its emotional outcomes.

## Employer optics
- All founder content speaks as an individual builder. No employer names, logos, office references, or "we at ___" constructions — including in comment replies and DMs.
- No posting from employer devices/accounts; no implying employer endorsement.
- The founder story is "I built a tool for my own social life" — it needs nothing else.

## Privacy language (use verbatim when sharing links)
> "Everything stays in your browser — no account, no contact import, it never reads or sends your messages."

## Platform-specific
- **Reddit:** disclose you built it whenever the product comes up. No fresh accounts, no fake personal claims, no posting the same thing across subs, no product talk in support communities (divorce, anxiety) — ever, even if asked publicly (answer generically, offer nothing).
- **Facebook groups:** read each group's promo rules; use the softer version by default in week 1; support-group posts carry no CTA at all.
- **Testimonials:** real, permissioned, behavior-focused quotes only. Placeholder quotes are always labeled as examples.
- **App Store (later):** see `../../app-store-readiness/07-app-review-risk.md` — same rules, higher stakes.

## Disclaimers (footer for landing/YouTube where fitting)
> Orbit is a planning and habit tool. It is not therapy, medical advice, or a mental-health treatment, and it doesn't promise friendships, dates, or any relationship outcome. It gives you a system for going out, talking to people, and following up — the part that's up to a tool. The rest is up to you, which is exactly how it should be.

## Bio-safe descriptions (pre-cleared for profiles)
- "A private system for rebuilding your social life."
- "Weekly outings, follow-up drafts, and a tracker for the people worth keeping."
- "Not a dating app. Not a feed. Just the loop."
