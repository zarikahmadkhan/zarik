## Weakest ~20% — flagged, with disposition

1. **Statics 28–30 ("Social rep of the day")** — weakest class members; the format needs a *series cadence* to work (daily posting), which the 30-day calendar doesn't commit to. **Disposition: hold** — don't post until you decide to run a daily-rep series; as one-offs they're filler.
2. **Statics 06, 17 (quote cards)** — solid lines but lower standalone stopping-power than their video versions. **Disposition: demote** — use as FB-page filler, not IG-feed leads.
3. **`videos/19` (Orbit in 30 seconds)** — text-only frames undersell a product demo; it *tells* where a screen-recording would *show*. **Disposition: revise later** — post the current version once (week 3 per calendar), then remake in CapCut as a screen recording using the same script/scenes; the package's script is written to survive that transfer.
4. **`facebook/fb19` (organizer post)** — most speculative audience; organizers are a research channel more than a conversion one. **Disposition: keep, reclassify** — treat replies as user research, expect no signups.
5. **`carousels/09` (divorce)** — good, but slide 3's "It's not fair. It's just true." runs close to the pity line the brand avoids. **Disposition: revised inline** — softened footer already; keep an eye on comment sentiment before reposting.
6. **AI-sounding risk (3.5–4 across image classes)** — the em-dash-heavy, aphorism-forward style reads authored-but-uniform at high volume. **Mitigation adopted in calendar:** never post two quote-cards back-to-back; alternate registers (mirror/default); founder-voice FB posts break the uniformity.
