The default outcome is isolation — and the 90-day protocol that changes it.
Orbit is a private system for rebuilding your social life: weekly outings, follow-up drafts, and a tracker for the people worth staying in touch with. No feed, no matching, no performing. Free to try — link in the pinned comment.

Not a dating app. Not therapy. Just the loop: go out, talk to one person, follow up, repeat.
