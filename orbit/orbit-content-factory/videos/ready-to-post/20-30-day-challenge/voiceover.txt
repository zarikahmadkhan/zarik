The thirty-day social rebuild challenge. Week one: leave the apartment — solo-but-public counts, motion over meaning. Week two: start conversations — one question per outing, learn one name. Week three: follow up — capture the weak tie, send the message. Week four: build cadence — pick your anchors, become a regular. Miss a day? Continue. Restarting is a trap. Thirty days, one ordinary rep at a time.
