## Description
I built a private app around the go-out → follow-up → repeat loop, because meeting people was never my problem — the second interaction was.
Orbit is a private system for rebuilding your social life — weekly outings, follow-up drafts, a tracker for the people worth keeping. Free beta, no account, data stays in your browser. Link in the pinned comment.

Not a dating app. Not therapy. Not an AI companion. Just the loop: go out, talk to one person, follow up, repeat.
