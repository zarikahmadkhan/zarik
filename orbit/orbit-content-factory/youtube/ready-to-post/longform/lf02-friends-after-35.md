## Outline & section scripts
### The automatic era ended (0:00)
School/college/early-career made friendship ambient: forced proximity + repetition + shared context, for free. After 35 all three must be manufactured.

### The math got worse (2:00)
Full calendars, home-default partners, kids, remote work. Nothing repeats unless someone makes it repeat. It's operationally annoying, not emotionally hard.

### What still works (4:00)
The three ingredients haven't changed — proximity, repetition, context. Recurring events supply all three: run club, class, community night, volunteer shift.

### The follow-up gap (6:30)
Even with anchors, ties die of symmetry — both people wait. The 72-hour rule and the five-word message.

### The 90-day protocol (8:30)
Two anchors weekly + one follow-up weekly + one invitation weekly. Boring. Compounds. What week 12 looks like.

### Tools (10:30)
Brief honest mention of Orbit as the system runner. CTA.
