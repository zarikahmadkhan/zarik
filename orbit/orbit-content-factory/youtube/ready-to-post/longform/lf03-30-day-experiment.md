### Rules of the experiment (0:00)
The four themes: leave the apartment / start conversations / follow up / build cadence. Miss a day → continue. Nothing heroic allowed — assignments are deliberately tonight-sized.

### Week 1 log (1:30)
Leaving the apartment is genuinely the whole assignment. The dread-vs-reality gap. Minimum viable outings that counted.

### Week 2 log (3:30)
One question per outing. 'How did you find this event' field test. The organizer conversation. What flopped.

### Week 3 log (5:30)
The follow-ups: sent, answered, ignored. The two-unanswered stop rule in practice. Reviving one dormant tie without apologizing for the gap.

### Week 4 log (7:30)
Anchor selection: what earned a permanent slot, what got dropped. The first unprompted invitation FROM someone else — the metric that matters.

### Scorecard + protocol download (9:30)
