# Adult friendship needs infrastructure, not motivation

**Thumbnail text:** Friendship is an / INFRASTRUCTURE problem

## Opening hook (first 20 seconds, verbatim-ready)
We treat friendship like a feeling and then wonder why it doesn't survive contact with a calendar. It's infrastructure — and infrastructure can be built.

## Outline & section scripts
### The category error (0:00)
Motivation-framing vs. infrastructure-framing. You don't 'feel like' having running water; you build plumbing.

### The three pipes (2:00)
Proximity (places you go), repetition (cadence that repeats), context (something to do together). Audit your week against all three.

### Why willpower keeps failing (4:30)
Willpower is a spike; infrastructure is a baseline. The saved-events folder as motivation theater.

### Building each pipe (6:00)
Anchors for proximity+repetition; structured formats for context; the follow-up habit as the pipe between meetings.

### The maintenance schedule (9:00)
Weekly minimum: one outing, one follow-up, one anchor visit. Sustainable beats impressive.

### Tooling (10:30)
Orbit as the infrastructure layer. CTA.
