# How to become a regular somewhere (the visit-by-visit playbook)

**Thumbnail text:** Become a regular. / Visit by visit.

## Opening hook (first 20 seconds, verbatim-ready)
Visit one you're a stranger. Visit three people expect you. Visit five you're getting invited to the after-thing. Here's the exact playbook between those visits.

## Outline & section scripts
### Why regulars win (0:00)
Mere exposure in plain language. Expected people get greeted, noticed missing, invited.

### Picking the right place (1:30)
Weekly cadence, same-people attendance, conversation-possible format, survivable alone. Scoring your candidates.

### Visits 1-2 (3:30)
Just attend; learn the rhythm; 'good to be back' to anyone familiar. No networking agenda allowed yet.

### Visits 3-4 (5:30)
Names — learn two, use them. Arrive early. The organizer conversation ('paid in hellos').

### Visit 5+ (7:30)
Small contributions: help set up, bring the thing. Helpers become insiders. The unprompted invitation as the graduation signal.

### Multi-anchor strategy (9:30)
Two anchors max, weekly, tracked. Orbit's repeat-exposure counter. CTA.
