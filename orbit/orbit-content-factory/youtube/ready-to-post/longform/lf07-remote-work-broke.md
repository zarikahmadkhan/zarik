# Remote work quietly broke your social life — here's the mechanism

**Thumbnail text:** Remote work broke / something invisible

## Opening hook (first 20 seconds, verbatim-ready)
Nobody voted to delete their social life. We just went remote — and the building that had been doing our social reps for us stopped showing up.

## Outline & section scripts
### The invisible service (0:00)
The office as ambient social infrastructure: 5-10 free micro-interactions daily. Nobody counted them because nobody paid for them.

### The deletion (2:00)
Slack is not contact; meetings are not conversation. The three-day no-conversation streak as the tell.

### Why coworking isn't the fix (4:00)
Zoom calls near strangers. The office's magic was repeated proximity + a reason to talk — proximity alone rebuilds nothing.

### The designed replacement (5:30)
Two evening anchors weekly + one solo-but-public work session + one follow-up. Mapping each to what the office provided.

### The transition week (8:00)
Realistic first week: it feels forced, then it doesn't. The rep-tracking frame.

### Tooling + CTA (9:30)
Orbit as the replacement's operating system.
