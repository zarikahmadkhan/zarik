# Moving cities as an adult is socially brutal (the 90-day integration protocol)

**Thumbnail text:** New city. / Nobody's coming.

## Opening hook (first 20 seconds, verbatim-ready)
Eight months in and nobody to call isn't a failure — it's the default outcome of an adult move. Nobody integrates you. Here's the protocol that does it deliberately.

## Outline & section scripts
### The default outcome (0:00)
The city doesn't notice you arrived. Work-from-home removed the last automatic pipeline. Naming the 8-month wall.

### Why the usual moves fail (2:00)
App-hopping for strangers, 12 different events (all cold starts), waiting for work friends. Each fails the repetition test.

### Days 1-30: anchor selection (4:00)
Two recurring anchors — one fitness-ish, one interest-ish. Attend each twice. Nothing else required.

### Days 31-60: familiarity (6:00)
Keep showing up. Names, 'good to be back,' the organizer. The city starts shrinking to rooms where your face repeats.

### Days 61-90: conversion (7:30)
One follow-up weekly to someone you've met twice. One invitation to the thing you already attend. First non-work plan on the calendar = graduation.

### Tooling + CTA (9:30)
Orbit ships with the protocol built in.
