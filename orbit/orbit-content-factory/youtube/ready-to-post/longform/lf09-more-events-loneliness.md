## Outline & section scripts
### Discovery is solved (0:00)
Meetup, Eventbrite, Instagram, city newsletters. Infinite inventory. The 'still home on Friday' paradox.

### The option-action gap (2:00)
Optionality feels like wealth; unattended options expire worthless. Saved: 14, attended: 1. Research as avoidance with a clipboard.

### What options can't do (4:00)
Options don't create repetition (novelty treadmill), don't create follow-up, don't lower the door-cost. The three things a social life actually needs.

### The one-decision rule (6:00)
No new saves until the last one is decided. Decide by 6pm. Let the night be ordinary.

### From browsing to a loop (7:30)
The full loop as the replacement for the browse habit. What a week looks like.

### Tooling + CTA (9:00)
Orbit forces the decision — statuses are interested/planned/attended for a reason.
