**Thumbnail text:** How I'll know / if this FAILED

## Opening hook (first 20 seconds, verbatim-ready)

## Outline & section scripts
### The claim under test (0:00)
Orbit's bet: a private system can change social behavior — outings, follow-ups, repeat attendance. Not feelings. Behavior.

### The north star (1:30)
Weekly completed social reps; secondary: follow-ups sent after real interactions. Why vanity metrics (downloads, views) are excluded.

### Phase 0: me (3:00)
Dogfood week with real events and real people. What I'm logging. The 'feels fake' list.

### Phase 1: 5-10 testers (5:00)
The four exit-interview questions. 'If it disappeared: relieved/indifferent/annoyed' as the only rating that matters.

### The kill criteria (7:00)
What failure looks like: testers onboard but don't return week 2; reps don't move; 'indifferent' majority. Pre-committing to the criteria publicly.

### The paid smoke test (9:00)
$9 intent test before real billing. Why payments-before-behavior-loop is the classic mistake. CTA to join the test.

## Description
The public test plan: north-star metrics, exit-interview questions, kill criteria, and the paid smoke test. Building without self-delusion.
Orbit is a private system for rebuilding your social life — weekly outings, follow-up drafts, a tracker for the people worth keeping. Free beta, no account, data stays in your browser. Link in the pinned comment.

Not a dating app. Not therapy. Not an AI companion. Just the loop: go out, talk to one person, follow up, repeat.
